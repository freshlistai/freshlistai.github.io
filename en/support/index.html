FAQ

The common questions.

Not here? Email - we add new answers as they come in.

How do I export my data?
In the app: Settings → Privacy → Export my data. You'll get a ZIP via email with CSVs of every item, recipe, and savings entry.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your items and recipes from the device, deletes your account records from our server, takes you out of any family, and signs you out. It's immediate and can't be undone.
Why can't I use the AI features?
AI features run on-device and need an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence (iOS 27). Turn it on in iOS Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri; right after you do, the model may need a little time to download. Without it, you can still add items by barcode or by hand.
I missed an item in my photo capture - what now?
On the review screen after capture, tap Add item at the bottom to add anything the AI missed. Or use Manual add from the fridge tab any time.
Notifications aren't showing up.
Make sure notifications are allowed for FreshListAI in iOS Settings → Notifications → FreshListAI. Then in-app: Settings → Notifications → make sure expiry reminders are on.
iCloud sync doesn't seem to be working.
Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → make sure iCloud Drive is on, and FreshListAI is enabled. If you signed in with Apple's private relay, your data still syncs to your iCloud - just under that stable identifier.
Do I need to buy or restore anything?
No. FreshListAI is free - there are no subscriptions, trials, or in-app purchases, so there's nothing to buy or restore.
How does household sharing work?
Create a household in the app and invite family by code - up to 5 people per household. It's free for everyone and managed entirely in the app.
Where do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email berinshteyn@gmail.com. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when it happened, if you can. Every email gets a reply.
