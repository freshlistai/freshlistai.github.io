Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Translations into languages other than English are machine-generated and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or missing, and some text may still appear in English. The English version is authoritative and controls in any conflict.

This Privacy Policy explains what FreshListAI collects, why, and your choices. We keep what we collect to the minimum needed to help you waste less food. By using the App you agree to this policy.

What we collect & why

  • Apple identifier: required for Sign in with Apple so we can recognize you. Apple may give us a private relay email; we never see your real Apple ID email unless you choose to share it.
  • Your fridge data: item names, quantities, expiry dates, photos you attach, recipes, shopping list, and savings. This is stored locally on your device and synced to your own private iCloud (CloudKit) account.
  • Photos: grocery photos and nutrition-label photos are analyzed on your device by Apple Intelligence. They are not uploaded to our server or to any AI provider.
  • Account & notifications: when you sign in, your Apple identifier, your display name (so it can be restored if you reinstall, since Apple only shares it once) and — if you allow notifications — an Apple push token are sent to our server so we can recognize your account and send expiry reminders and (if you use it) family notifications. This happens whether or not you use Family Sharing.
  • Family Sharing (optional): if you create or join a family, we store on our server a family code, your display name, your member identifier, and the shared shopping list / fridge items (including small photo thumbnails) so your family can see them.
  • Product & nutrition lookups: barcodes you scan and product or food names you look up are sent through our server to product and nutrition databases — Open Food Facts, UPCItemDB, USDA FoodData Central, and FatSecret — to fetch product details, images, and nutrition facts. No AI is involved in these lookups.
  • Device verification: to protect our servers from abuse, the App uses Apple's App Attest to confirm requests come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App. This registers an anonymous device key with our server; it is not linked to your identity and carries no personal data.
  • Server logs: like any online service, our server and our host (Cloudflare) automatically process your device's IP address and basic request details to deliver responses and guard against abuse. These logs are kept only briefly.
  • How-to videos: when you open a cooking step's how-to video, a short technique keyword (for example "julienne") is sent to our server, which searches YouTube (Google's YouTube Data API) for a matching video. The video then plays from YouTube under Google's privacy policy.
  • Notification settings are stored locally and notifications are scheduled on-device.
  • Siri & Shortcuts (optional): when you ask Siri "what's expiring" or "what's in my fridge," the answer is read from the fridge data already on your device and spoken back to you. This feature is read-only and sends no additional data off your device.

AI processing (Apple Intelligence, on your device)

Every AI feature — identifying groceries in photos, reading nutrition labels, estimating expiry dates and nutrition, and creating recipes, meal plans, and ingredient swaps — runs on your device with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device foundation model. The photos, text, and fridge contents these features use are processed on your iPhone and are never sent to us or to any AI provider. On-device Apple Intelligence processing is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

AI features need a device that supports Apple Intelligence with it turned on. On other devices they're unavailable, and you can still add items manually or with the barcode scanner.

Where your data lives

  • On your device (Apple SwiftData) and your private iCloud account (CloudKit), which we cannot read.
  • On our Cloudflare-hosted server: product and nutrition lookup requests and their cached results, your account record (Apple identifier, display name, push token), and, if you use Family Sharing, your shared-family records.
  • With Apple (Sign in with Apple, iCloud, App Attest, push notifications, and on-device Apple Intelligence), Open Food Facts, UPCItemDB, USDA FoodData Central, FatSecret, YouTube (Google), and Cloudflare (our server host), each under their own privacy policies.
  • Outside your region: these providers are based mainly in the United States, so your data may be processed in the US and other countries whose data-protection laws may differ from yours.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising or ad tracking, ever. No IDFA.
  • No location, contacts, or microphone access.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data retention

Local and iCloud data stays until you delete it or remove the App. Photos used by AI features stay on your device; we never receive them. The Apple identifier, display name, and push token stored on our server, and any Family records, persist until you delete your account or leave/delete the family; push tokens are refreshed on each launch. Server request logs (including IP addresses) are kept only briefly for security and then discarded.

Your rights & controls

  • Delete your data: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Export your data: Settings → Export my data.
  • Turn off permissions (camera, notifications, photos) anytime in iOS Settings → FreshListAI.

Your privacy rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & California)

Wherever you live, you can access, correct, export, and delete your data using the in-app controls above, and you can email us to object to or restrict processing or to withdraw consent.

EU / UK (GDPR): we process your data to perform our contract with you (to provide the App) and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working. You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your personal information, and have not in the past 12 months. The categories we handle are identifiers (your Apple identifier, display name, and push token), user content (the food data and photos you provide), and internet/usage data (IP address and request logs). You have the right to know, access, correct, and delete this information and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights, and we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.

Children

FreshListAI isn't directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent where you live), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes & contact

If we change how data is handled, we'll surface a notice in the App. Questions or requests: berinshteyn@gmail.com.
