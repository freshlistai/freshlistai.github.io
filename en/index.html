Capture

One photo. The whole haul.

Lay groceries on the counter. Snap once. Apple Intelligence identifies every item, right on your iPhone - name, quantity, where it lives, when it expires. Scan a barcode for instant nutrition.

  • Barcode scan → instant nutrition (FatSecret, USDA, Open Food Facts).
  • Reads the printed best-by date off the package.
  • On-device AI - your photos never leave your phone.
Use Soon

Quiet reminders, two days before.

We nudge you exactly when it matters - with a recipe that uses what's about to go bad. No spam. No daily check-ins.

  • Smart expiry estimates - location- and opened-aware.
  • Fridge / freezer / pantry locations.
  • Swipe to mark used or wasted - use just some if you like.
Recipes

Cook with what you have.

Tonight's hero recipe uses items expiring first. Plan a few days at once, swap ingredients you're out of, and Cook Mode goes hands-free - auto-playing a how-to clip for every step. Even the macros are real, from a nutrition database.

  • AI swaps + Plan my meals (3-5 at once).
  • Recipe macros from a nutrition database, not a guess.
  • Cook Mode: auto-playing how-to video per step.

FAQ

The honest answers.

Still wondering? Email berinshteyn@gmail.com - every email gets a reply.

Does it work without internet?
Mostly, yes. Your fridge, expiry tracking, and reminders are all local, and the AI runs on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence - so photo capture works offline too. Barcode lookups, how-to videos, and household sharing need a connection.
What happens to my grocery photos?
They stay on your iPhone. Apple Intelligence identifies your groceries on-device - photos are never sent to our server or to any AI provider.
Can I share my fridge with family?
Yes, for free. Household sharing lets up to 5 people share one fridge and shopping list, with real-time sync and an activity feed.
Is it really free?
Yes. FreshListAI is completely free - no subscriptions, no trials, no in-app purchases, and no ads. Every feature is included for everyone, and we never ask for payment details.
iPad or Android?
iPhone and iPad on iOS 17 or newer, in portrait or landscape. Android isn't on the roadmap yet.
Do I need Apple Intelligence?
Only for the AI features - photo identification, nutrition-label reading, expiry and nutrition estimates, recipes, meal plans, and ingredient swaps. They run on devices that support Apple Intelligence (iOS 27) with it turned on. On other devices you can still add items by barcode or by hand, and the rest of the app works the same.
