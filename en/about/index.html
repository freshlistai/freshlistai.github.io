Why

We built it because we needed it.

The average household throws away a meaningful chunk of grocery spend every year - not on purpose, but because nobody remembers the spinach in the back of the crisper. FreshListAI is the calm, quiet helper we wanted for ourselves.

  • No analytics, no ads, no data sale - ever.
  • AI runs on your iPhone - photos never leave it.
  • Your fridge data lives on your device + your iCloud.
