Features

Built to disappear
into your week.

Five tabs. One photo. Nothing to manage. Here's everything the app actually does - no marketing fluff.

CaptureFridgeUse SoonRecipes & Cook ModeSavingsHouseholdLanguagesNotificationsPrivacySiri & Shortcuts

Roomy on iPad - portrait or landscape, iPhone too.

Capture

One photo. The whole haul.

Lay groceries on the counter. Snap once. Apple Intelligence identifies every item, right on your iPhone - name, quantity, where it lives, when it expires. Scan a barcode and it pulls the product and full nutrition instantly.

  • On-device identification with Apple Intelligence
  • Barcode scan → instant nutrition (FatSecret, USDA, Open Food Facts)
  • Reads the printed best-by date off the package (OCR)
  • Adjust quantity before saving (e.g. 3 apples)
  • Manual add fallback always available
  • Photos stay on your iPhone - never uploaded
Fridge

Everything you bought, in one place.

Your inventory, organized by category and storage location. Search, sort, filter by date - SwiftData under the hood, optional iCloud sync.

  • Storage locations: fridge, freezer, pantry
  • Tap any item for nutrition facts & macros
  • Mark it opened → the date shortens to the safer after-opening window
  • Used only part? "How many did you use?" keeps the rest
  • Search and multi-filter
  • Photo, category, brand, and notes per item
Use Soon

What to cook, this week.

A focused list of items expiring within your window - with matching recipes attached to each one.

  • Configurable expiry window
  • Suggested recipes per item
  • Swipe to mark used or wasted
  • Tracks money saved automatically
Recipes & Cook Mode

Cook with what you have.

Recipes are ranked by what you have AND what's expiring first. A 'Tonight' hero card highlights the one to cook now. Plan a few days at once, swap ingredients you're out of, and cooking quietly updates your fridge. Even the macros are real - looked up from a nutrition database, not guessed.

  • Hero recipe based on expiring items
  • Recipe macros from a nutrition database (FatSecret) - not an AI guess
  • Plan my meals: 3-5 recipes in one tap
  • AI ingredient swaps from what's already in your fridge
  • Scale servings up or down - amounts recalculate
  • Cook Mode: full-screen, hands-free, timers - one step at a time
  • Each step auto-plays a how-to clip and shows the fridge item to grab (expiry + amount)
  • Cooking deducts what you used; rate, save & history
Savings

The number that matters.

Every item you don't waste is counted. By month, by year, all-time. Plus a no-waste streak.

  • Three views: month / year / all-time
  • Compare to reference metrics ("a week of groceries")
  • Shareable end-of-month card
  • No-waste streak counter
  • Full history, kept forever
Household

Built for shared kitchens.

Up to 5 people share one fridge, synced live across everyone's devices - free for everyone. Invite by text. Anyone can mark items used or wasted.

  • Real-time household sync
  • Invite-by-code (text or iMessage)
  • Co-owner and member roles
  • Activity feed of who did what
  • Shared shopping list, grouped by category
  • Mark purchased → batch add to fridge
Languages

Speaks your language.

The app localizes into 13 languages, following your device or an in-app picker you can change any time. Translations are machine-generated and improving (beta).

  • 13 languages, including Arabic (right-to-left)
  • Auto-detects your device language
  • Switch any time in Settings
  • iPhone & iPad, portrait or landscape
Notifications

Quiet reminders, two days before.

Local on-device notifications. Default lead time is two days, fully configurable - including quiet hours so we don't ping you at 6am.

  • Scheduled on-device - no push server
  • Configurable lead days and reminder time
  • Quiet hours (default 22:00 - 07:00)
  • Per-item smart timing
  • Turn off any time in iOS Settings
Privacy

Local-first. Yours, not ours.

Data lives on your device in Apple's SwiftData store, and the AI runs on-device with Apple Intelligence. Optional CloudKit sync uses your iCloud account - not ours.

  • Sign in with Apple (private relay email)
  • AI runs on-device - photos never leave your phone
  • Zero analytics, zero ads
  • Delete or export your data anytime
  • iCloud sync is opt-in, your account
Siri & Shortcuts

Ask, don't tap.

Ask Siri what's about to expire or what's in your fridge and hear the answer read back - hands-free. It's read-only, so Siri never changes your fridge, and it answers from your own on-device data.

  • "Hey Siri, what's expiring in my fridge?"
  • "Hey Siri, what's in my fridge?"
  • Read-only - Siri never edits your fridge
  • Answers from your own on-device data
  • Rename the phrase in the Shortcuts app
  • Voice answers are English for now
